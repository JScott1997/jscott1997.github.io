Puzzlegeist

Unity · C# · TortoiseSVN

Puzzlegeist is a puzzle game where players must help Gray the ghost escape his haunted manor by possessing objects with chess inspired movement patterns. I was a programmer on this project, responsible for implementing the game's core mechanics and animation systems, as well as having QA responsibilities.

Canadian Fraud and Cybercrime Analysis

Python · Pandas · Dash · Plotly

My colleagues and I analyzed a Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre dataset from 2021 - 2025 to uncover patterns in reported fraud, financial losses, and victim demographics across Canada. We created a dashboard to visualize our findings and provide insights into the most common types of fraud, the regions most affected, and the demographics of victims.
